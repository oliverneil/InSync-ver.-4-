InSync Consulting Services ("InSync," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you visit insynconline.net, use our staffing services, or interact with our team.

By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Contact information — name, email, phone number, physical address
  • Employment information — resume, work history, certifications, skills, and availability
  • Business information — company name, job title, staffing needs, hiring preferences
  • Healthcare credentials — licenses, certifications, compliance documentation (for healthcare placements)
  • Communications — messages, emails, or other content you send to us

We also collect information automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time on site, and referring URLs
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

2How We Use Your Information

  • Match candidates with relevant job opportunities
  • Connect employers with qualified talent
  • Process applications and manage placements
  • Communicate about open roles, interviews, and onboarding
  • Verify credentials and conduct background checks (with consent)
  • Send service updates and relevant communications
  • Improve our website and services through analytics
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Employer clients — candidate profiles shared with employers seeking to fill open roles, with your consent
  • Service providers — background check providers, ATS platforms, payroll processors, and other vendors operating under confidentiality agreements
  • Compliance authorities — credentialing bodies, Joint Commission, or regulatory agencies as required
  • Legal authorities — when required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

4Data Retention

We retain candidate and employer information for as long as necessary to maintain our working relationship and comply with applicable laws. Candidate profiles may be retained for up to seven (7) years to facilitate future placements. You may request deletion of your information at any time (subject to legal retention requirements).

5Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request updates to inaccurate or outdated information
  • Deletion — request removal of your personal data from our systems
  • Opt-out — unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format

To exercise your rights, email us at privacy@insynconline.net. We respond within 30 days.

6Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies for website functionality, analytics (Google Analytics), and marketing. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

7Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, access controls, and regular security reviews to protect your information. No internet transmission is 100% secure, but we take every reasonable precaution to safeguard your data.

8International Transfers

InSync operates primarily within the United States. If your information is transferred internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

9Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18. If you believe we have collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Significant changes will be communicated via email or a website notice.
