Health Savings Account (HSA)

InSync offers employees a Health Savings Account (HSA) through Lively. An HSA is an individually-owned account used to save and pay for qualified medical expenses, paired with a qualifying high-deductible health plan. For 2026 you can contribute up to $4,400 (individual) or $8,750 (family), plus an extra $1,000 if you’re 55+.
